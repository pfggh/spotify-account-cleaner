  3. Under Redirect URIs, add the EXACT URL shown above (e.g. http://localhost:5173/).
  4. Go to User Management (or Users & Access) and add your Spotify email address (Required by Spotify for newly created apps in Development Mode to prevent HTTP 403).
  5. Click Save at the bottom of the page.
